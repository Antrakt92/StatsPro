Antrakt UI Sans is a modified font, not an official Noto or Droid release.

It combines Noto Sans with the complementary character coverage of
Droid Sans Fallback Legacy. Noto glyphs win overlapping Unicode assignments.
Droid hints are removed before scaling its outlines from 256 to 1000 UPEM.
Font layout tables are merged and the font is renamed. The build preserves
the conservative merged metrics. See README.md and sources.json.
